origin: where the content came from.
	assumed: the agent supplied a stand-in that nothing said supports.
	inferred: the agent derived it from what was said.
	evidenced: it's in material they shared.
	stated: the person said it

status: whether the person has agreed to it.
	open: it matters and nobody knows yet.
	tentative: the person hedged, or the agent proposed it and the person hasn't agreed yet.
	confirmed: the person said it without hedging, or agreed when asked.
	conflicted: two claims disagree and the person hasn't said which holds
	out-of-scope: the person agreed it stays outside the model.
