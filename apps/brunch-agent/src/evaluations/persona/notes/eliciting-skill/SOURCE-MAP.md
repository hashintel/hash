# Source map: `manual/skills/eliciting/SKILL.md`

Where each fragment of the `manual` arm's eliciting skill came from, and what it was trying to say. This maps the skill as copied in `39c3ff9941`, which is still the `identity` and `receipt` arms' version. The `manual` arm's skill has since been recomposed; see [`ANALYSIS.md`](./ANALYSIS.md).

## Lineage

The skill is the fourth compression of one body of work:

1. **Research, August 2026.** `docs/research/elicitation/` in the lib, removed from the repo in #9810 (recoverable at `9e9f72a96e^`):
   - `elicitation-strategy-literature.md`: simulation-modelling and cognitive-task-analysis literature. Objectives first (Robinson, Sargent), Critical Decision Method incident sweeps, the ACTA knowledge audit, the premortem, quantiles over min/mode/max, a small quiver of patterns rather than a long menu.
   - `interviewing-literature-source-catalog.md`: requirements-interview literature. Goguen & Linde's say–do problem, the Ferrari et al. interview-mistake taxonomy and ambiguity-as-cue line, Gause & Weinberg's context-free questions, and contextual inquiry's "when was the last time that happened?".
   - `frontier-model-elicitor-failure-catalogue.md`: FM-01 to FM-15, observed failures of model interviewers.
   - `elicitation-research-synthesis-2026-08-31.md`: the adjudicated summary.
2. **Drafts, 1–2 September.** `packages/core/_drafts/ampcode/` and `_drafts/five-register-synthesis/`, deleted in `79529cb12b` and `da9f926ebb` (recoverable at their parents). The five-register draft sorted everything under five headings: Directives, Recognition, Operations, Coverage and Verification. Its `SOURCE-MAP.md` traces each heading to the research and to the predecessor Brunch `elicit` skill.
3. **Lib skills.** `packages/core/src/skills/elicitation/SKILL.md` holds the universal half, plus the Ledger discipline. `packages/plugin-sdcpn/.../references/profile.md` holds the operational-process half, a merge of the ampcode and five-register profiles. Together they run about 5,300 words, with a named heading per move.
4. **App skill, 30 September** (`e62b8a269b`, copied into `manual/` in `39c3ff9941`). Both lib files are fused into about 1,350 words. The five registers are renamed Directives, Cues, Moves, Information needs and Verification. Each bullet merges two to five lib headings into one sentence.

The headings the compression deleted were the activators: "Slice a concrete case", "Deposit and defer", "Silent hardening" and so on. What remains is the content without the names that said what each piece was for.
