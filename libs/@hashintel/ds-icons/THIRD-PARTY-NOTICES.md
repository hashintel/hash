# Integration artwork notices

Brand names, marks, and logos belong to their respective owners. The package's MIT/Apache licenses apply to its implementation; they do not grant rights to third-party trademarks or replace the owners' brand-use terms.

The existing HASH integration artwork comes from `hashintel/internal-sites` (AGPL-3.0). Additional vector artwork comes from the brands' sites and the linked archives. The `gilbarbara/logos` collection uses CC0; `pheralb/svgl` and `devicons/devicon` use MIT. Those collection licenses do not grant trademark rights. The package preserves the artwork's provenance below.

Gmail full-logo files embed authentic PNG artwork. Microsoft Teams combines its product mark with a vectorized wordmark from an official Microsoft PDF. Veeva Vault pairs its mark with the Veeva corporate wordmark. BigQuery and Redshift use archived product lock-ups. Apple Notes has no verified full-logo asset and exposes only mark and tile variants.

Celonis, Veeva Vault, Oracle, Messenger, Instagram, Zoom, and GitHub lock-ups combine the sourced mark and wordmark; they are compositions, not downloaded complete lock-ups. The Celonis and Oracle marks omit their embedded lettering to avoid repeating the name. Their original mark assets remain available in the mark and tile variants.

Word, Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneNote use Microsoft's October 2025 product marks, distributed via Wikimedia Commons (marked PD-textlogo there). Their full-logo compositions use the product names outlined in [Microsoft's Segoe UI Semibold 5.32](https://c.s-microsoft.com/static/fonts/segoe-ui/west-european/Semibold/latest.woff2). They follow the Teams layout and light/dark text treatment; they are not downloaded official lock-ups. Only the lettering outlines ship, not the font.
