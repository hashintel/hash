# Ledger Filing for Operational Processes

The `ledger_commit` catalogue lists this model's categories. They are filing homes, not interview order: follow the person's thread and do not read categories aloud as a questionnaire. A category being available does not make it mandatory; a consequential omission becomes an open Note where it matters.

File each Note once, under the most specific category that fits, and refer to related Notes by id instead of restating them. Keep the person's wording, your normalization, uncertainty, conditions and contextual variation inside the Note. If a load-bearing fact is recoverable only from the transcript, the Ledger is incomplete at that point.

## What goes where

- **`purpose`**: what the model must answer, compare or support; who will use it and how; boundary, horizon and accuracy expectation; available time and appetite for assumptions; what the result must not claim.
- **`operational`**: cross-cutting operational context that belongs nowhere more specific.
- **`operational/goals`**: goals, measures, constraints and decision thresholds, at the precision the person can judge.
- **`operational/boundary`**: triggers, prerequisites, initial state, calendars, and what is inside or outside.
- **`operational/resources`**: participants, locations, flowing things and resources: counts, availability, eligibility, and how the operation treats them differently.
- **`operational/activities`**: one activity's local account per Note: prerequisites, performer, inputs, outputs, duration, success and failure. For each load-bearing input, say whether it is consumed or transformed, reserved and later released, or read while remaining available. An activity's place in the case belongs in the process spine.
- **`operational/process-spine`**: the ordered account of a concrete case in the person's vocabulary: what admits it, which activities occur in what order, what decides branches, where it waits and why, what failure and recovery do, and what outcome or handoff ends it. Cite activity and resource Notes by id rather than repeating their details. Add a contrasting case only when it exposes structure the first does not.
- **`operational/quantities`**: durations, rates, counts, arrivals and probabilities, each with the item, activity, mode, direction, load, location or calendar that selects it.
- **`operational/policies`**: posted and practiced rules, tie-breaking, overrides and exceptions, with the conditions selecting each.
- **`operational/validation`**: observations, records or judgments that could test the model, and where evidence remains unavailable.
- **`open-matters`**: only an unresolved matter that affects several Notes or needs a later return path. Name the affected Note ids, what remains unresolved, what it prevents, and what would re-enter it. A gap local to one concern is an open Note in that concern's category.
- **`construction`**: once construction begins: stand-ins and what would replace them, representation choices and revisions, inferences, labelled defaults, approximations, and target-representation losses, each naming the Note ids it rests on. Record what was built or changed, not that the net cannot carry something.
- **`delivery`**: what the account currently supports, consequential gaps, and net status: construction not attempted, blocked, partial, or tool-schema accepted; whether the inspected definition was structurally reviewed against the account; and whether behavior was untested, observed in named simulations, or established to a stated scope by stronger analysis. Supersede the previous delivery Note rather than adding a parallel one.
