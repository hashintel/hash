# SDCPN Construction-Readiness View

Read this only after construction has been requested and before choosing SDCPN mappings. Read the current domain-primary workpiece first. This view projects authoritative operational knowledge toward construction; it is not another account of the operation, an elicitation checklist, or evidence that a net will behave correctly.

## Authority and location

Write the view under the workpiece's existing `Construction notes`. Cite exact workpiece heading paths and compact claim names. Do not copy operational descriptions, values, rules, evidence, or epistemic annotations into the view. The cited operational account remains authoritative.

Use the view to record only:

- which workpiece references bear on an applicable SDCPN concern;
- the resulting construction obligation or candidate concern;
- a missing distinction that permits materially different representations;
- an anticipated capability limit or representation loss that mapping must confirm.

When readiness work reveals a new operational fact, update that fact at its domain-primary authoritative location and leave only a reference here. When it reveals a cross-cutting unresolved matter, use the issue ledger according to its locality rule and reference that entry. A construct-only conversation cannot obtain new expert evidence: record the gap and smallest re-entry question instead.

## Build the projection

1. Select the smallest construction slice that bears on the stated simulation use. Reference its objective and authoritative case/process-spine material.
2. Walk the applicable lenses below. For each one, cite the workpiece locations that support a representation, or cite the location that should own a missing fact.
3. Record only the construction obligation, gap, or anticipated loss. Leave operational content at the cited location and defer concrete mappings, inferences, approximations, and defaults to `references/pn-construction.md`.
4. Block only the unsupported path when a missing distinction admits materially different structures. Unrelated supported structure may proceed when the current runtime can construct it safely.
5. Read `references/pn-construction.md` to choose mappings and `references/checks.md` to inspect the result. The readiness view does not replace either resource.

The projection is complete enough to begin the selected construction slice when every load-bearing construction obligation traces to an authoritative workpiece reference, and every known gap that would materially change that slice is blocking or explicitly routed to re-entry. An inference, approximation, default, capability limit, or loss cannot substitute for a missing operational fact.

## Readiness lenses

These are construction addresses, not required headings and not question order. Omit a lens that cannot affect the selected use or slice.

### Intended use and observable consequence

Reference the question, comparison, decision, objective dependencies, and validation evidence that select what the net must expose. Record only the corresponding construction scope or a gap in observable support.

### Flowing cases, carried state, and distinctions

Reference what flows and any type, context, direction, mode, location, or state distinction that changes behavior. Record which distinctions any representation must preserve without translating every participant or location into an independent node; defer the concrete mapping to the construction resource.

### Boundary and scenario state

Reference admission triggers, prerequisites, initial populations, calendars, arrivals, external events, and terminal outcomes. Record what must be available to initialize or drive the selected slice; defer whether it belongs in core net structure, scenario state, or parameters.

### Activities, events, and occupancy

Reference consequential activities and events, including preconditions, inputs, outputs, state changes, duration, failure, and what remains occupied while work is in progress. Record whether timing or occupancy must be explicit; do not reproduce the activity account or choose its concrete factoring here.

### Ordering, enabling, branching, and joining

Reference the authoritative process spine, branch and join conditions, hidden waiting causes, retries, recovery, and handoffs. Record the enabling, selection, synchronization, or recovery semantics a mapping must preserve, or identify the missing condition that makes them ambiguous.

### Input and resource semantics

Reference each load-bearing consumed, reserved/released, or read relation and any capacity, availability, acquisition, changed-state return, contention, or terminal loss. Record the availability and return obligations a mapping must preserve, and flag any reusable resource whose release semantics are absent.

### Policy and selection

Reference practiced priorities, tie-breaks, overrides, eligibility, release rules, and the context selecting among them. Record the selection semantics a mapping must preserve. Preserve unsupported or conflicting policy as a gap rather than compiling a convenient rule.

### Time, stochastic choice, and continuous change

Reference consequential durations, distributions or qualitative variation, probabilities, rates, thresholds, resets, units, and selecting context. Record which quantitative or dynamic distinctions a mapping must preserve; keep unsupported precision visible as a gap or approximation and defer the concrete representation.

### Representation and capability limits

Reference operational material the target or mounted tools may not faithfully carry. Record an anticipated loss and affected objective under `Target-representation losses` for confirmation during mapping; flag unsupported updates or removals rather than assuming an additive workaround.

## Reference-only shape

Populate the workpiece's existing `Construction notes` headings with this reference-only shape; do not add a parallel readiness section. Omit empty groups rather than manufacturing content.

```markdown
## Construction notes

### Candidate target structures

- **Selected slice** — references: <objective and process-spine locations>; scope: <what part of the intended use this construction bears on>
- **<applicable readiness lens>** — references: <authoritative workpiece locations>; consequence: <construction obligation or candidate SDCPN concern>

### Questions reopened by construction

- **<gap>** — authoritative home: <where the answer belongs>; consequence: <materially different structures still possible>; re-entry: <smallest operational-language question or named evidence source>

### Target-representation losses

- **<anticipated loss>** — references: <authoritative workpiece locations>; possible limit: <target or capability constraint for mapping to confirm>; consequence: <affected construction scope or objective>
```

This view is a navigation aid, not a score, closed readiness state, duplicated summary, or promise that every target concern has been populated.
