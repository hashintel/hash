# Workpiece, Construction, and Delivery Checks

Read this when preparing to construct, after construction changes, and before delivering a net. Interactive elicitation verification lives in the universal and SDCPN elicitation references.

These checks support judgment; no external harness computes completion. Readiness is purpose-relative, and a useful result may remain partial.

## Before construction

- The workpiece states at least one purpose, question, comparison, or decision in the person's terms.
- Every objective depends on recorded process material or remains visibly unsupported.
- A cold reader can recover what flows, what admits it, the main activities and their order, what changes the path, which resources are occupied, and what outcome ends or hands off the case.
- Consequential participants, locations, resources, policies, constraints, quantities, and contextual regimes are represented or visibly unresolved.
- Failures relevant to the purpose state what happens to the work and resources, retry scope, surviving state, recovery, and terminal result.
- Precise claims have evidence or visible agent authorship; prescribed and practiced accounts are not silently merged.
- Assumptions, unknowns, conflicts, deferrals, omissions, and target losses remain distinguishable where present.

If materially different nets remain possible because one consequential operational fact is absent, construction is blocked at that fact. Name the smallest missing distinction in the person's vocabulary. Ask it only when interactive elicitation is available; otherwise report it for re-entry.

## During construction

- The latest client-owned net was inspected before mutation.
- Every target element has support in the workpiece or a recorded construction inference.
- Tool payloads follow the mounted schemas rather than examples remembered from prose.
- Each dependent stage is inspected before later elements rely on it.
- A rejected tool call is corrected or left as an explicit blocker; it is not treated as a successful change.
- Construction does not reread the transcript to bypass an incomplete workpiece.

## Structural validity

- The resulting net has at least one place, one transition, and an ordering path capable of progressing a case.
- Every arc endpoint exists and each arc weight is a positive token multiplicity.
- Mutually exclusive outcomes are structurally or conditionally exclusive rather than encoded with zero-weight arcs.
- Timed work, where required, has distinguishable start, occupied or in-progress state, and completion.
- Shared reusable resources are unavailable while held and return on the supported success, failure, cancellation, and recovery paths.
- Consumed, reserved, and read-only inputs have materially different target semantics where the workpiece requires them.
- Retry paths return to the supported scope with the supported surviving state.
- Every dynamic quantity that remains in the net affects a guard, event, metric, or other consequential behavior.

A parser-valid empty or dead net fails this check.

## Fidelity and uncertainty

- Activities occur in the order and under the conditions the workpiece claims.
- Resource counts, capacities, eligibility, acquisition, release, and changed return state match the workpiece.
- Branch rules and probabilities retain their evidence, precision, and selecting context.
- Directional mode-change and spatial-transfer effects have not been made symmetric without support.
- Prescribed rules have not silently replaced practiced behavior.
- Construction choices, approximations, parameters, defaults, and collapsed distinctions are recorded in the workpiece.
- Unsupported objectives, unconnected data bindings, qualitative validation, and other target losses remain named rather than hidden by parser validity.

## Existing-net revision

- The pre-change net was inspected and the desired semantic delta stated before mutation.
- Changed elements are traceable to changed or disputed workpiece material.
- Unrelated structure remains untouched unless its meaning actually depends on that change.
- An add-only toolset has not simulated replacement by adding competing structure beside obsolete elements.
- Unsupported update or removal operations are reported instead of claimed.

## Delivery

Deliver the current workpiece in all cases and the client-owned net only when mounted tools produced and checked one.

State plainly:

- what was elicited, revised, constructed, inspected, or checked;
- what the result can support and what it must not claim;
- which assumptions, approximations, unresolved gaps, and target losses remain;
- whether construction was not attempted, blocked, partial, or completed through the mounted path; and
- the smallest consequential question or capability needed next, if any.

Do not replace that account with a closed outcome label, infer simulatable status from a full-looking workpiece, or open a new topic after the person elects to stop.
