# Process-Model Workpiece and Recording Contract

The workpiece is the shared, recoverable operational account. Elicitation and revision maintain it; construction consumes it. The transcript remains evidence, not the primary model.

Follow the person's thread during conversation and file material into the workpiece afterward. Its headings are recording homes, not question order. Use only the sections relevant to the stated purpose, while keeping consequential omissions visible.

## Recording distinctions

Use these distinctions wherever they carry information; they are not mandatory labels on every sentence:

- **Expert evidence** — What the person said, preserving consequential vocabulary, hedges, context, and source regime.
- **Working account** — The current normalized operational description accepted for use.
- **Agent inference** — An interpretation of evidence, visibly attributed to the agent.
- **Assumed** — A value or structure proposed by the agent, with its reason, consequence, and route to checking.
- **Unknown** — Asked, but the person does not know.
- **Not yet asked** — Relevant and identified, but not yet addressed.
- **Deferred** — Left for later, with why it matters and a named source or route to an answer.
- **Declined** — The person chose not to answer or include it.
- **Conflict** — Accounts cannot yet be used together; preserve both and what could distinguish them.
- **Correction** — A current account that replaces an earlier one, retaining enough history to explain the change without leaving both active.
- **Contextual coexistence** — Different accounts apply under different people, times, modes, directions, policies, or operating conditions.
- **Omitted** — Deliberately outside the model, with why the purpose permits the omission.
- **Target loss** — Supported operational meaning the SDCPN or current tools cannot faithfully carry.

Evidence and precision are independent. “About three hours” may be supported but coarse; an exact agent-supplied value may be precise-looking but unsupported.

Keep one authoritative home for each active claim. Do not accumulate competing summaries. When a later statement differs, establish whether it is a correction, conflict, or contextual coexistence before updating the working account.

## Maintenance

- Prefer the person's terms for names and process descriptions.
- Update the authoritative process spine when ordering or case behavior changes; reference activity and resource entries rather than duplicating their local details.
- A restatement offered by the agent remains normalization even when accepted. Preserve exact evidence separately where origin matters.
- For a genuine correction, update the active account and record what changed. For conflict or contextual coexistence, keep the alternatives and their unresolved or selecting conditions visible.
- Remove irrelevant empty sections. Use **Not yet asked**, **Unknown**, **Declined**, **Deferred**, or **Omitted** only when that state matters to later work.
- If construction requires transcript archaeology to recover a load-bearing fact, the workpiece is incomplete at that boundary.
- Record whether no construction was attempted, construction was blocked, a partial net exists, or a net was constructed and checked through mounted tools. Do not infer simulatable status from workpiece quality alone.
