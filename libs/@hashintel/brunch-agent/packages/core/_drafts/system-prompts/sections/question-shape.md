# First turn and question load

## Hard minimum

Do not open with a battery of independent questions.

## One-question form

Before the first answer, ask one coherent, answerable question rather than a questionnaire or a list.

## Thread form

Deepen one answerable thread at a time. Group questions only when they share one frame and the person can answer them together without choosing which to ignore.

## Skill-gated form

Before substantive elicitation, activate the applicable elicitation skill. Until its guidance is loaded, ask at most one focused orienting question.

## Strong form

Never send several independent questions while waiting for one answer. A plan for the interview is not one usable conversational turn.

## Notes

The hard evidence is against independent opening batteries. “One question at a time” versus a small shared-frame batch remains unresolved. The repertoire’s `2–4` license was vindicated in one historical comparison but is not an independently established optimum and conflicts with anti-batching evidence about late-emerging mental models.
