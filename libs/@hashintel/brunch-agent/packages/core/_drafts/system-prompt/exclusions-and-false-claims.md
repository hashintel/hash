# Exclusions and false claims

Do not copy the following into the generic core system prompt.

## Plugin and target content

- SDCPN, Petri nets, Petrinaut, places, transitions, tokens, colours, firing, construction tools, or simulation-specific investigation.
- Gherkin features, rules, examples, or executable-specification semantics.
- `runbook-ir`, a fixed IR heading tree, workpiece emission syntax, or target-specific sufficiency.

## Suspended machinery

- “The harness keeps/folds the model,” computes demanded slots, decides completion, or computes an affected slice.
- `brunch_ask`, `brunch_sweep`, pending-affordance state, quote extraction, capture application, or typed completion cues.
- Stable assertion IDs, projection manifests, provenance joins, observer folds, or scoped net patches.

These may describe prior implementations or future work, but they are not facts about the active production path.

## Evaluation controls

- Hidden truth ledgers, scenario answers, expected fact IDs, grader criteria, mistake IDs, weighted scores, hard-stop turn counts, token budgets, or model/provider configuration.
- Claims that a question count, turn count, or acquisition score proves quality.

## Unsettled technique choices

- `2–4` as a universal batch size.
- A mandatory numeric objective or threshold.
- One universal quantitative script.
- Always clarify directly versus always deepen through a case.
- A fixed number of orientation questions or mandatory first-five script.
- A full read-back versus concise gap-led close.

## Overbroad identity

- “Expert process-model elicitor.”
- “Modelling assistant inside Petrinaut.”
- Any claim that every person is an expert or every target is a model.

## Misleading convenience

- “Use sensible defaults” or “make it up” without explicit agent authorship, reason, and check method.
- “Complete every category” when categories are target-specific and sufficiency is purpose-relative.
- “Ask what you failed to ask” as proof of coverage; a clearinghouse question cannot reveal an omission neither side recognizes.
