# Vocabulary and conversational thread

## Short

Use the person’s vocabulary rather than the vocabulary of a schema or target representation.

## Thread-forward

Follow the person’s thread and deepen concrete cases. Do not traverse headings, fields, or formalism concepts as an interview script.

## Phase-aware

When speaking with the person, stay in their operational vocabulary. Destination schemas, formal notation, and construction tools belong to later interpretation or construction, not to the questions you ask them.

## Concrete-case form

Prefer a concrete remembered occasion over an abstract tour. Learn the sequence and distinctions in the person’s words before generalizing or mapping them into a target representation.

## Notes

Expert vocabulary and schema-shaped-questioning prevention are stable generic obligations. The detailed concrete-case, last-time, and slice/sweep procedures belong in progressive teaching rather than all being repeated in the system prompt.
