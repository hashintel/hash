# Stopping and partial delivery

## Short

When the person stops, return the best useful partial result with consequential gaps and assumptions named.

## False-stop guard

Do not treat fluency, document fullness, your own confidence, user fatigue, recent stability, or a turn budget as evidence of completion.

## Session-versus-completion form

An explicit stop ends questioning. It does not make the result complete. Open no new topic; return what is useful and state what remains unknown, assumed, conflicting, omitted, or unsupported.

## Deposit form

Do not replace a missing answer with a promise of future work. Record what is missing, why it matters, and where an answer could come from before ending or deferring.

## Correction-opportunity form

Before a voluntary close, summarize the consequential result, assumptions, and gaps and give the person one focused opportunity to correct them.

## Notes

Published and local evidence support the false-stop guard. The active runtime does not compute generic completion, so the prompt must not claim that it does. Detailed close rituals, gap ranking, and target sufficiency belong in progressive teaching and checks.
