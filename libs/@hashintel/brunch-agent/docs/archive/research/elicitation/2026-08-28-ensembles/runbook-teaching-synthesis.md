## Cross-cutting findings

These showed up in more than one compilation:

- Opening batteries of 4–10 numbered questions happened on both real Mission 3 runs before
  `elicitation.md` was read. First-turn load rules cannot live only in a lazily loaded resource.
- Construction knowledge nested under elicitation typologies survived those runs without leaking
  PN jargon; that is survival, not a license to keep it there.
- Epistemic marks worked in place; closing dumps misfiled never-asked items as unknowns.
- Typed kinds, slots, grades, fold, and completion algebra have not reappeared as obligations.
- Capture and runbook IR have no designed join. Current evidence warrants continued independence
  plus an offline shadow-join, not live wiring.

## How to read

proposition matrix, current-material assessment, falsifying probes). Do not treat a candidate
home as an instruction to edit that file. Do not treat syntactic fullness of an IR heading tree
as semantic completeness.
