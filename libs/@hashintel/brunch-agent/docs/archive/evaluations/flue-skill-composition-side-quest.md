activation with packaged universal disclosure. The owner set aside the side quest's
Candidate B fallback and directed Mission 4 to implement the independent capability
topology. V3 remains bounded evidence that independent activation was unreliable in
that instrument (0/3) while packaged disclosure routed more often on S1 (2/3);
neither topology passed the complete cross-scenario condition.
