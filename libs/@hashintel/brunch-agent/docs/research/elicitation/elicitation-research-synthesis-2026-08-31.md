## Method and confidence

Each corresponding pair of part reports was compared before either ensemble overview was read. The comparison separated shared claims, unique contributions, genuine contradictions, and unresolved calls. Only after the four part-level comparisons were complete were the two overviews assessed against that record.

The ensembles largely synthesized the same local source pool. Repeated wording across them is therefore not independent corroboration. Confidence rises where distinct evidence classes align: verified literature, observed Mission 3 behavior, current teaching, and the elicitation-to-IR oracle's obligations.

The historical Mission 3 runs remain calibration evidence, not a replicated baseline. They can identify plausible failures and candidate interventions, but they do not establish comparative effectiveness. The frozen prospective three-run baseline has not yet been executed.

## Executive conclusion

The evidence does not justify a larger elicitation architecture. It justifies three bounded experiments:

1. Repair phase and placement failures in the current runbook shape.
2. Compare two stronger organizations for the Markdown runbook IR.
3. Measure capture-to-IR evidence relationships offline without joining the production systems.

The production capture pipe and runbook IR should remain independent. The most plausible future seam is not a shared artifact or capture fold; it is a narrow relationship among evidence support, epistemic treatment, lifecycle, and projection loss. That relationship must first be measured offline.

## Shared elicitation model

The interview is objective-relative evidence acquisition, not a traversal of headings.

- Establish the decision, comparison, or worry; audience; boundary and horizon; accuracy need; non-claims; and tolerance for assumptions.
- Begin with one concrete remembered occasion and walk it from trigger to end before generalizing.
- Deepen where the stated objective makes a distinction consequential. Do not collect exhaustive process trivia.
- Keep expert statements, agent inferences, assumptions, unknowns, not-yet-asked items, conflicts, corrections, omissions, and losses distinguishable.
- Treat divergence as information. Do not silently reconcile policy with practice, two contextual truths, or a genuine correction.
- Spend questions by information value: cheap independent breadth may be batched, but load-bearing threads should be deepened coherently.
- Stop on evidence-bearing criteria, not fluency, heading fullness, user fatigue, turn count, or model self-report.
- Use the expert's operational vocabulary during elicitation. Petri-net topology, schemas, distributions, and tool mechanics belong to construction.

### Universal behavior still needing discriminating probes

The research does not settle these choices:

- Where the first-turn anti-overload invariant must live: resource, skill body, or system instruction.
- Whether one question at a time is necessary, or a small shared-frame batch can be safe.
- How much posture to collect before the concrete case without recreating an intake form.
- When ambiguity should trigger direct clarification versus deeper case-based probing.
- The right quantitative and tail-question script.
- Whether closing should be a concise gap-led correction opportunity or a broader read-back.
- How assent to agent-authored wording confirms meaning without becoming independent user evidence.

Both ensembles underdeveloped anti-leading behavior, vague-question detection, exception and absence sweeps, expert answerability, declined/deferred/not-yet-decided outcomes, objective-specific accuracy, and the question “Are you correcting that account, or do both apply under different conditions?”

## SDCPN-specific investigation

A minimum useful investigation is an objective-relevant slice with named holes, not a complete typology checklist. It should establish:

- the objective, audience, boundary, horizon, and what the model must not claim;
- one concrete walked case with a reconstructable trigger, spine, and end;
- what flows, prerequisites, main activities, ordering, and outputs;
- for load-bearing inputs, whether each is consumed, reserved then released, or merely inspected;
- scarce-resource capacity and practiced contention rules where simultaneous demand matters;
- practiced branch decisions, alternatives, retries, failures, recovery, overrides, and conflicts;
- timing, waiting, arrivals, grouping, directional losses, thresholds, and changing condition only where they can change the stated answer;
- expert evidence, assumptions, unknowns, conflicts, omissions, corrections, and projection limits;
- validation evidence and the operational alternatives the expert wants compared.

### Adjudicated SDCPN calls

- A numeric threshold is not universally mandatory. Require a judgeable objective or measure; require a number only when the expert uses one or the objective depends on it.
- Do not add external arrivals as a default typology yet. Strengthen the demand/arrival trigger and promote it only after repeated prospective misses.
- Condition-dependent duration, failure, and loss rates are a real blind spot, but should first enter as the probe “Does its state change how long or how often?” under properties, time, or continuous-change guidance.
- Hidden waiting belongs in a strengthened Timed work lens before it becomes another typology.
- Complete the existing Grouped movement teaching; it is thinner than its peers.
- Unclear release of a scarce reserved resource is a blocker when alternate release semantics can change the objective. Otherwise it may remain only as a conspicuous assumption.
- Move the `Transform to PN` children out of the expert-facing elicitation resource and consolidate them in construction-owned guidance.

A short construction-to-elicitation return list is a strong candidate, but not yet a proven runtime completion system: missing objective, missing spine, unclear scarce-resource fate, policy without practiced contention, missing arrival account under a throughput objective, or missing tail behavior where tails matter.

## Runbook IR obligations

The runbook IR must let a cold reader reconstruct the intended process, its load-bearing assumptions and unresolved conflicts, and the smallest next question without rereading the transcript or inventing the spine.

Mission 3 exposed several structural strains:

- Both real IRs were composed wholesale near the end; incremental maintenance was taught but not exercised.
- Epistemic labels did not reliably preserve authorship. Agent-authored values could read like expert testimony, and never-asked material could be filed as user unknowns.
- `Situation notes` helped retrieval but also duplicated more authoritative sections.
- `Projection losses` mixed elicitation gaps, construction decisions, and true representational losses.
- Assumptions often lacked the required reason and check method.
- Conflict and supersession information could be duplicated or contradicted by closing summaries.
- Quantities need their operational basis: measured thing, direction or context, typical versus tail, range or hedge, and incident versus rate.
- Resources, eligibility, calendars, contention, and directional losses must be locatable together.

### Candidate IR organizations

These are experiment families, not settled designs:

1. **Phase-separated current shape:** retain the current broad organization while separating expert-given content, agent assumptions with reason and check method, elicitation gaps, construction decisions, and construction-opened losses. This is the lowest-change control.
2. **Process spine with overlays:** use one walked-case/process spine as the backbone, with attached resource, quantity, rule, branch, and directional-change overlays. This is the strongest first redesign candidate because it reduces constructor reassembly while preserving context.
3. **Objective slices, cases, then residue:** organize around the questions the model must answer, supported by cases, evidence, assumptions, blockers, validation, and smallest next questions. This most directly tests objective-relative completeness.
4. **Light epistemic ledger:** use a small overlay for authorship, conflict, correction, and gap retrieval. Do not make it the constructor's primary semantic model without evidence that prose-plus-phase separation fails.

An append-only journal is not currently warranted. The project has not observed enough live correction and maintenance behavior to justify its cold-reading and construction burden.

## Capture and runbook IR

Keep the model-free capture pipe and Markdown runbook IR independent in production.

The capture pipe retains immutable, idempotent evidence envelopes. The runbook IR is an editable semantic workpiece currently rewritten wholesale. They have different roles and lifecycle semantics. Turning either into the other would restore unproven typed extraction, fold semantics, or in-loop judgment associated with Condition 5 latency.

The next evidence step is an offline shadow map over an existing or future elicitation run. Stable persistent IR statement ids are not required for this one-run analysis; temporary evaluation ids suffice. Stable identity becomes necessary only if links must survive rewrites or regeneration.

The map must keep orthogonal dimensions separate:

```text
evidence relation: direct | multi-capture synthesis | unsupported
epistemic treatment: explicit | inferred | assumed
lifecycle relation: active | corrected | superseded | conflict
projection treatment: represented | omitted | loss
```

Measure evidence support, assumption marking, synthesis fan-in, context dependence, correction integrity, capture utility, path sensitivity, and link churn across successive IR emissions. Do not report assumptions as evidence support. Do not treat the final IR fence as sufficient evidence of revision lifecycle.

Support links are the only plausible deeper seam currently earned as a hypothesis. A live link feature would still require a statement-identity and rewrite-stability proof. A capture fold or one-artifact merger remains unsupported.

## Assessment of the archived overviews

The archived cross-mission digest is the richer planning artifact, but it overstated several conclusions: shared-source agreement as independence, numeric thresholds as universal minimums, two new typologies as already justified, stable statement identity as necessary for offline links, equal compaction risk for capture and IR, mutually exclusive shadow-map categories, and the exact equivalence of a one-artifact merger to Condition 5. It also became stale when it claimed the part matrices lived only in a transcript.

The archived runbook-teaching overview is better calibrated but too compressed to serve as a synthesis. Its five cross-cutting claims are sound, especially the first-turn placement failure, survival-not-proof of misplaced construction teaching, closing-dump epistemic failures, non-return of typed machinery, and continued capture/IR independence. It omits most unresolved calls, structural alternatives, and discriminating probes.

This report therefore uses the detailed digest's breadth, the concise overview's restraint, and the cold pairwise adjudications rather than selecting either ensemble wholesale.

## Recommended evaluation sequence

Do not edit the teaching before executing the frozen prospective baseline. Otherwise the project loses the clean comparison point it just built.

1. Run the unchanged three-replication baseline under `evaluations/protocols/prospective-runbook-v1/`.
2. Grade each run with independent omniscient and cold contexts and human-adjudicate hard failures, grader disagreements, and new mistake classes.
3. Compare a **phase-discipline repair** bundle: a pre-first-question load invariant, construction material moved out of elicitation, stronger authorship/assumption treatment, and elicitation gaps separated from construction losses. Keep the current typology count.
4. Compare a **process-spine with overlays** bundle.
5. Compare an **objective-slices** bundle if the first redesign does not dominate or if it exposes a different useful trade-off.
6. Only after one coherent bundle wins, ablate one layer at a time to identify the effective intervention.
7. Run capture/IR shadow mapping offline; do not add a runtime join to any teaching variant.

Keep the model, expert pack, hard stop, graders, and replication count fixed across comparable campaigns. Report dimension vectors and hard failures rather than choosing by one mean total.

### Highest-value additions to the probe set

- The same situation under two different objectives.
- Equivalent evidence presented in different orders.
- A true correction after the interview appears construction-ready.
- Agent-authored wording followed only by expert assent.
- A suitable question that the simulated expert cannot answer.
- A condition that changes a duration or rate but crosses no threshold.
- Successive full IR emissions, to observe maintenance and link churn.
- An ambiguity where direct clarification and case-based deepening compete.
- A non-empty, parser-valid net whose simulated behavior contradicts the IR.

## Current health implications

The project has a real end-to-end tracer through the Flue agent, skill activation, resource reads, headless interview, recoverable Markdown IR, and Petrinaut parser boundary. The evaluation harness is substantially stronger: the ruler and prompts are frozen, historical artifacts have been calibrated, and the prospective runner is ready.

It is not yet load-bearing at the construction boundary. The only real-model validated-tool run produced an empty net after nine provider-schema rejections, and parser acceptance proved vacuous. The construction-discovered-gap return, incremental IR maintenance, path robustness, host/session continuity, live Petrinaut read/write, and semantic simulation fidelity remain unproven.

The immediate planning risk is therefore not lack of research. It is allowing elicitation refinements, construction integration, host continuity, and deadline-driven demo work to compete without a declared delivery contract. The next mission cut should be chosen against that contract, not against the attractiveness of the research backlog.

Before reshaping `MISSION.next.md`, settle:

1. What observable outcome must exist by the delivery date: research evidence, a reliable elicitation experience, a generated importable net, live canvas mutation, session continuity, or a rehearsable demo story?
2. Which failures are acceptable in that delivery: manual import, a fixed case, no capture join, no compaction proof, or no semantic simulation check?
3. Is the date a stakeholder review, a code-complete boundary, or a production/demo event?
4. How much paid-model experimentation and human adjudication is available before then?
5. Which current future missions are truly prerequisites for that outcome, and which can wait without reducing its credibility?

Until these are answered, the research supports running the frozen baseline and preserving the current production boundaries, but it does not by itself determine whether host continuity or typed Petrinaut construction should be the next delivery mission.
