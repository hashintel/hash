# Vestera prospective runbook baseline v1 — campaign adjudication

## Verdict

The frozen v1 campaign has three paid invocations: one invalid runtime member and two complete, independently graded members. The valid members establish a useful control range, not a pass threshold: omniscient totals `66.3–80.0 / 100`, cold utility `3.3–3.5 / 4`, and downstream readiness `conditional` in both. Neither valid run fired a hard-failure gate.

The baseline therefore supports a split conclusion. The runbook path can produce grounded, cold-usable, explicitly partial workpieces through the production Flue agent without foreground sweep/capture/construction machinery. It does not do so robustly enough to treat one run as representative: objective-aligned acquisition varied by a full score point, load-bearing operational rules remained unasked in the weaker run, and one of three invocations failed before the simulated expert produced any text or the runner retained its normal evidence.

This campaign establishes Mission 3's elicitation/workpiece control and closes no part of its already-falsified real-model construction claim.

## Mistake and intervention observations

No valid run produced `INVENT`, `HARDEN`, `CONFLICT-COLLAPSE`, `UNSUPPORTED-COMPLETE`, `OPENING-OVERLOAD`, `SCHEMA-QUESTIONING`, or a `NEW-*` class. The absence of prospective opening overload is notable because both historical calibration runs exhibited opening batteries; with only two valid members it is evidence against treating that historical pattern as deterministic, not proof that placement/dosage needs no redesign.

The dominant finding class was acquisition miss. Both graders also found that the workpieces named many gaps but did not rank the smallest next questions by downstream consequence. Cold readers judged the artifacts navigable and epistemically strong, while still only conditionally ready because important parameters or policies would require explicit assumptions.

Replication 3's omniscient report used six `SIM-NONDISCLOSURE-*` labels even though it also states that the interviewer had not yet asked the ledger's revealing tacit probes. Under the frozen taxonomy, `SIM-NONDISCLOSURE` requires a suitable question that the simulator failed to answer. The campaign adjudication therefore treats those six rows as mixed/uncertain acquisition misses rather than simulator nondisclosure. This is a grader-application correction, not a new taxonomy class.

No genuine cross-grader disagreement requires disposition. The cold reviews' stronger utility judgments and the omniscient grader's lower readiness in replication 3 are expected role differences: the latter can see undisclosed ledger facts, while the cold reviewer can judge only the explicit partial artifact.

## Strong behavior to preserve

- Concrete-case entry and adaptive deepening rather than mechanical traversal of IR headings.
- Explicit correction handling: replication 3 caught and preserved the expert's white-to-tint versus tint-to-white washdown correction.
- Preservation of estimates, hedges, unwritten rules, expert uncertainty, and unasked material without unsupported completion claims.
- Clear objective, process spine, resource constraints, and named partial boundary that a cold reader could reconstruct.
- One skill, progressive resource reads limited to `elicitation.md` and `ir-template.md`, no capture write, and no construction resource in the elicitation campaign.

## Redesign inputs, not automatic edits

The owner-led redesign mission should examine rather than blindly implement these pressures:

1. How to spend the eight-turn budget so shifts, contention policy, due-date semantics, minimum runs, and objective weights are reached or explicitly prioritized without restoring an opening battery.
2. How to make the smallest next questions and their downstream consequence visible instead of presenting a long undifferentiated `Not yet asked` inventory.
3. How to reduce repetition and reader effort while keeping the concrete case, epistemic marks, and contextual quantities reconstructable.
4. How to distinguish simulator silence, questions not yet asked, and genuinely expert-unknown material more reliably.
5. How to retain whole-workpiece synthesis off the ordinary-turn latency path and how to preserve evidence when any provider call fails before normal artifact persistence.

The baseline does not by itself select a case spine, epistemic ledger, objective slices, versioned assertions, or another workpiece shape. Those remain design alternatives to work through with the owner.

## Dispositions

- **Replication 1:** retain as invalid; do not replace. Count it in campaign validity and artifact-recovery rates. The missing failure artifact is an instrument limitation to fix only in a later protocol version.
- **Replications 2–3:** accept as valid baseline members. Both used source commit `b738aa1be1a62a9f9cdde89ced78558f04293a77`, the frozen model configuration, eight turns, hard stop, canonical resources, and no test overrides.
- **Hard failures / `NEW-*`:** none; no mandatory human gate disposition was triggered.
- **Run 3 `SIM-NONDISCLOSURE` rows:** do not add them to simulator-failure counts; retain the grader report unchanged and record the adjudicated interpretation here.
- **Baseline claim:** accepted as a two-member gradable range inside a three-invocation campaign with one invalid runtime member. It is sufficient as a control for a versioned redesign comparison, but not as a universal performance estimate.
