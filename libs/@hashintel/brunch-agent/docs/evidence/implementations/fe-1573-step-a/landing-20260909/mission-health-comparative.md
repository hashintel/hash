## Verdict

**Agree with the sibling on outcome; disagree on what the volume is and what caused it.** Substantial local mechanical capability; mission outcome still unproved; the genuine Vestera question remains unasked. That is the right product diagnosis.

The sibling treats the size problem mainly as an effort asymmetry: many implementation and verification lines before the first genuine run. That asymmetry is real and is not enough. Versus `main` the branch is **7,908 files, +397,687 / −20,760**. About **368k of those added lines, in 7,695 files**, are retained evidence packets. Tests are about **22k**. Production app source is about **2.2k**; the paid-run evaluation driver is about **2.4k**. The PR looks huge because of archaeology and a second proving product, not because the construction/explanation camp is large.

The planning kernel that produced this is stronger than “agents wrote extra tests.” A1–A6 were used as parallel strata that each needed a throughline, oracle, freeze, review, and retained packet **before** A6. That inverts the expeditionary rule in [`AGENTS.md`](../../../../../AGENTS.md): a vertical tracer does not automatically require horizontal completion, and an exposed seam is not by itself a stratum to close. The live contract scheduled the product question last, then treated every exposed seam as load-bearing now. Chunks became ends because the authority encoded that order, not because the strategic mission was forgotten.

The mission is neither accepted nor demonstrated impossible. HTTP 401 is a credential/accounting stop, not an architecture or model falsifier. Passing thousands of synthetic tests is not positive evidence for the missing semantic claims. Those three sibling sentences stand.

**Health by dimension, restated for breadth:** local safety evidence is substantial within named bounds; functional breadth is partial and is the next automatic campaign if class tables remain “still required”; epistemic honesty has been maintained; mission direction is at risk for the sibling’s reason **and** because the retention/chunk form will keep spawning lanes unless authority is recut. Stop expanding while landing. Do not replace that diagnosis with a percentage-complete estimate.

This is a methodological choice: never overwrite original reds, pin every instrument, retain raw histories, replay on ALPHA, archive the archive. Parallel lanes multiplied it. It is not a measure of product capability and should not be read as one.

### 2. Chunks became ends because the contract scheduled them that way

The sibling describes direction risk as capability and proving infrastructure advancing faster than genuine-use evidence. True, and downstream of a prior choice.

[`MISSION.md`](../../../../../MISSION.md) grew from 243 lines / 50k bytes at the Step A cut (`c7cd25c59c`) to 356 lines / 113k bytes at landing. Line count barely moved; bytes more than doubled because Status and the execution graph absorbed every allocation, AFK decision, and lane limit. Agents implementing “against the mission” were implementing against a growing operations log.

A1–A6 were written as responsibility boundaries. In practice each chunk ran: synthetic throughline → counterexample → bounded fix → retain red → new discriminator → full portfolio → pin → review → integrate → amend authority. Construction class tables then listed remaining removals, arc variants, and parameters as “still required.” That is the next automatic campaign.

A6 — genuine scenario, behavioral discriminator, cadence/basis, useful coverage — remains “untested.” The Vestera case pack already existed. The prospective oracle named in the mission, `evaluations/oracles/vestera-scheduling/mission-7-behaviour.test.ts`, was never written. The product question was structurally last.

This is the inversion: survey → line → camp → next survey was replaced by “harden A1–A5, then ask whether Brunch can elicit Vestera.” The three laws already forbid treating a provisional line as a hardened departure base and fortifying every adjacent contract because one route exposed it. The live mission, as amended in flight, asked agents to do exactly that.

Until credentials and the outstanding hold are explicitly disposed, further harness growth cannot produce mission evidence. The already-authored current-A5 scenario remains a diagnostic, as the sibling says — and the harness around it is optional infrastructure, not the camp.
