## Earned boundary and limits

Unique unchanged text at the same UTF-16 span carries its relations, including overlapping declarations. Offset-changing renames, moves, paraphrases, splits, reworded merges, deletion and undeclared reintroduction do not automatically carry evidence. Duplicate passage wording prevents carry; duplicate headings alone do not. A successful edit with no carry is not a rejected edit. Explicit declarations establish new current-local relations, not authenticated passage continuity, source relevance or predecessor/successor identities.

Broad spans and deliberately reused in-bounds numeric coordinates over different text remain structurally admissible as explicit new declarations. They do not earn semantic support. The matrix exposes these limitations without inventing fields, heuristics or an identity graph.
