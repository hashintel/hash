# Sharing a Net

On the Petrinaut demo website, **Share** in the top bar creates a link that contains a copy of the net. Anyone with the link can open it in another browser, without an account.

## Share a snapshot link

1. Open the net and choose **Share**.
2. Leave **Include current view** on to open the snapshot where you are: the mode, scenario, subnet and selected item. Turn it off to open it at its default view.
3. Choose **Copy link** once the link appears.

The snapshot holds the net as it was when you opened the dialog: its title, layout, descriptions, code, parameters, types, subnets, scenarios and metrics. Running simulations, results, AI conversations and preferences are not included. Later edits do not change a link you shared, and deleting your copy does not revoke it.

Published examples and snapshots have no **Share** button: copy the address from the browser instead.

## Open a snapshot

A snapshot opens read-only, and opening one saves nothing in your browser. You can inspect the net, switch views and run simulations. Choose **Make a local copy** to save an editable copy at its own address, keeping the current view.

## Share a file instead

**Download file** in the Share dialog saves the net as a YAML file, which the recipient imports from **Menu > Import**. Use it when a net is too large for a link, in which case **Copy link** stays disabled, or when an app shortens long links.

If a snapshot will not open, ask the sender for the complete link or the file. A message about a newer link format means Petrinaut needs a refresh.
