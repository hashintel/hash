---
title: Reveals add steps
notes:
  default: >-
    The arrow keys step through a slide before moving to the next one. The
    slide counts its steps from the highest number its reveals and notes name;
    nothing declares the count.
  byStep:
    1: Reveal at 1 shows a block from step 1. Until then it keeps its space, so nothing moves.
    2: A Reveal inside a sentence shows only those words.
    3: until 3 hides the first block again.
    4: >-
      OnlyInline takes no space while hidden, so two of them can share one
      place: one until step 4, the other from it.
---

## Reveals add steps
