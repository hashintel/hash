## Terms glide to their new place

Solve for $x$:

$$
\swap{1}{
  \morph{a}{a}\,\morph{x}{x} + \morph{b}{b} = \morph{c}{c}
}{
  \swap{2}{
    \morph{a}{a}\,\morph{x}{x} = \morph{c}{c} - \morph{b}{b}
  }{
    \morph{x}{x} = \frac{\morph{c}{c} - \morph{b}{b}}{\morph{a}{a}}
  }
}
$$
