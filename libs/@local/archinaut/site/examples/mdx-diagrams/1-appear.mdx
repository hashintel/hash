  byStep:
    1: Compile carries at-1, and its connection comes with it.
    2: Bundle carries at-2.
    3: Serve carries at-3.

## Parts appear with `at-n`

```d2 A pipeline: slide files are compiled, bundled and served
direction: right

source: Slide files
compile: Compile {class: at-1}
bundle: Bundle {class: at-2}
serve: Serve {class: at-3}

source -> compile
compile -> bundle
bundle -> serve
```
