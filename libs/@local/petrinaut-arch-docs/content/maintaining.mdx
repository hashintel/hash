These pages are about the documentation system itself, not about using Petrinaut.

Everything under [Architecture](architecture) is generated from annotations in
the covered packages (`@hashintel/petrinaut-core`, `@hashintel/petrinaut`,
`@hashintel/petrinaut-cli`, `@local/petrinaut-python`, and
`@apps/petrinaut-opt`), joined against the real import graph. Editing a
generated page does nothing: the next build replaces it.

Two things follow from that, and they are what these pages cover.

- **[Declaring layers](doc:maintaining/declaring-layers)** — the two annotations
  that put a folder on the map, where to write them, and what the build refuses
  to accept.
- **[Running locally](doc:maintaining/running-locally)** — building the docs,
  reading them without a server, and what to do when a page looks stale.
- **[Previewing changes](doc:maintaining/previewing-changes)** — how a PR
  preview highlights the pages and blocks the branch changed, and how that
  comparison is built.

Pages like this one are hand-written. They live in
`libs/@local/petrinaut-arch-docs/content/` and are optional: the generator
produces a complete bundle with that directory absent. They carry the reasoning
an import graph cannot supply, and nothing checks them against the code, so they
avoid restating facts the generated pages already own.
