`optimizations-view.tsx` is the **Optimizations** tab of Simulate mode. It
reads the [optimizations provider](layer:react.optimizations)'s context and
renders one table row per record (name, scenario, objective with its
direction, steps finished over requested, best value, and a status chip),
with **Create** opening the create drawer
(`setSimulateDrawer({ type: "create-optimization" })`) and a row selecting the
record, which the navigation state carries as the simulate resource and the
view drawer shows. The tab is rendered only while `useOptimizationSource()`
yields a source: a remote capability, or a connected one with the experimental
**In-browser optimization** setting on.

## Status words

`optimization-status.ts` names a record's status once for the list's chip and
the frame's pill: `optimizationDisplayStatus` maps the record to a display
status (Initializing, Running, Paused, Complete, Error, and for `cancelled`
either **Cancelled** (remote) or **Stopped** (connected), because a stopped
connected study keeps its sampler and can be continued; **Reconnecting** while
a remote stream reconnects, whatever the status says), and
`OPTIMIZATION_STATUS_DISPLAY` gives each its word and tone, the chip colour
following the tone. `paused` is the simulation layer's word: a connected study
drained on request, its steps in flight landed, its sampler kept; the record
reads `paused` from the click and becomes resumable when the segment's
`paused` event lands.

## One study: drawer and full view

Both surfaces render the [shared results view](layer:ui.views.editor.results)
(`SimulateView/shared/results-view.tsx`): `ResultsView` draws a
`ResultsModel` inside the [frame](layer:ui.views.editor.drawer-frame) and reads
no record of its own. The experiment drawer renders the same view from
`experimentResultsModel`; what differs between an experiment and a study is
data and a few slots, not layout. `study-results.tsx` is the study's adapter:
the pure `studyResultsModel(record, dependencies)` maps an
`OptimizationRecord` onto the model, and `useStudyResultsModel` supplies the
dependencies from the optimizations provider (`setOptimizationNavigation`),
the editor (`setSimulatePresentation`) and the user settings
(`enableOptimizationSurface`). `view-optimization-drawer.tsx` renders the view
with `drawer` (a ds `Drawer`, `presentation: "drawer"`);
`optimization-full-view.tsx` renders it filling the section with **Back to
list** as `leading` (clears the simulate resource, `presentation: "full"`).
`optimizations-view.tsx` picks the surface from
`EditorContext.simulatePresentation` (the navigation state's
`simulatePresentation`, `drawer` or `full`, which the website's URL carries as
`present`): `full` with a selected record renders the full view in place of
the list and the drawer.

The model's header: the one-line title `studyTitle` (the name, then
`describeStudy`: the scenario, then `Maximize` or `Minimize` and the metric);
the `headline` slot holding `StudyHeader` (`study-results/study-header.tsx`),
`describeStudyProgress` (`SimulateView/shared/describe-study-progress.ts`,
shared with the sweep navigator; `Step 17 of 30 · best step so far: step 12 (650.5)`
while running; `Paused at 12 of 30 steps · 1 step finishing · …` with a grey
**Paused** chip while paused; `Finished`, `Stopped`, `Cancelled` or `Failed
after …` once settled) and, while running, a verdict chip from
`assessConvergence` (`study-results/convergence.ts`): best-so-far stagnation
over the last `convergenceWindow(requestedTrials)` completed steps,
`too-early`, `improving` or `converging`; the status pill (`studyStatus`, from
`OPTIMIZATION_STATUS_DISPLAY`, sized for `Reconnecting`); the stat columns
(`studyStats`): **Steps** as `describeStepProgress` (`4 / 30`, then `· 3 runs
each` and `· 2 at once` when those are above 1), **Steps clear** for a study
with constraints, and **Best step so far** with the best step's parameters as
its tooltip (`formatParameters`); the `activity` list for a connected study
(`activityBatches`, `describeBatch`: `Step 4`, or `Refining population=1850,
infected_ratio=0.36`), which the view draws as the `N computing` chip;
`compute` for a connected study only (the `ComputeBackendBadge`); the progress
bar as `stepsProgressPercent`; and the note row from `studyNote`: the error,
else the resume note while paused. `isOptimizationActive` decides what reads
as live; a settled study shows results and never a live title. The scenario
name, the objective metric's name and the `Maximize`/`Minimize` word come from
`SimulateView/shared/study-labels.ts`, which the list, the title, the objective
chart's subtitle and the surfaces share.

The model's body, for a connected study: one band, **Parameters**
(a `FrameCard`, its fixed parameters folded behind the footer button, with
`OptimizationNavigatorStatus` as its trailing line and
`OptimizationNavigator` as its content, both from
`study-results/optimization-navigator.tsx`); the surface slot holding
`NavigatedOptimizationSurface` when the study has two or more numeric axes;
the metrics grid with one tile, the objective at the point, titled
`objectiveAtPointTitle` (**Objective at the step in flight** while a live
study is followed, **Objective at the selected point** otherwise), named after
the objective metric in its subtitle and fed `connected.selection` (empty when
the selection carries an error; the navigator's status line carries the
reason), followed by the study's cards in the same grid: `ObjectiveHistoryCard`,
`ConstraintSummaryCard` when the study declares constraints, and
`ParameterImportancePanel`, every card `OBJECTIVE_PLOT_HEIGHT` tall; and the
`after` slot holding `StudySteps` (`study-results/study-steps.tsx`), the steps
table in a fixed 320 px box with the best step starred. A paused study is the
running layout with `tone: "paused"` on the grid and its cards.

For a remote study the model has no activity, no compute badge, a **Best
parameters** band from the first render (`ParameterValues` over the optimized
parameters, `—` until the first step reports, so the card is as tall before a
best as after), the experimental **Surface** card when the **Optimization
surface** setting is on and the study has two or more numeric axes
(`OptimizationSurface`, which walks a grid through `sampleDetachedObjective`;
[the sweep surface](doc:experiments/sweep-surface) covers it), no timeline
tile, the objective by step alone in the grid, and the steps table from the
first render too, empty until trials exist, none starred. Only a study
evaluated here receives importances, so a remote study has no Sensitivity
analysis card rather than an empty one. Nothing mounts mid-stream: the frame's
zero-layout-shift contract holds on the remote path as on the connected one.

`ObjectiveHistoryCard` (`study-results/objective-history-card.tsx`) is the
objective by step, the shared `ObjectiveHistoryChart`
(`SimulateView/shared/objective-history-chart.tsx`) in a card:
`buildObjectiveHistory` (`SimulateView/shared/objective-history-data.ts`)
orders the trials by step and threads the best so far through the completed
ones, `toObjectiveHistoryData` lays them out as uPlot aligned data
(`[steps, objectives, bestSoFar]`), and the chart draws the objectives as
dots and the best as a stepped line, in the palette and sizes its
`ObjectiveHistoryStyle` names, with an optional pinned right edge (`xMax`)
and dashed `dividers` between studies for the sweep's objective strip
([Studies started from a sweep](#studies-started-from-a-sweep)).
`trialFeasibility` reads a point's feasibility off the trial event's
`constraints`: `unknown` without results, `infeasible` for a draw pruned by a
parameter constraint. A pruned step has no objective, so it draws nothing,
and an `infeasible` step never becomes the best so far; the steps table marks
its state in `INFEASIBLE_COLOR` (`SimulateView/shared/infeasible-color.ts`,
Optuna's grey).

A study with constraints reports them through
`react/optimizations/constraint-rates.ts`, derived per render from
`record.trials`: `stepVerdict` (clear, limited, infeasible), `bindingStepRate`
(the step's lowest pass rate) and `studyConstraintRates` (steps clear over
steps simulated, per constraint too). `ConstraintSummaryCard`
(`study-results/constraint-summary.tsx`) follows the objective by step in a
connected study's grid, `studyStats` gains a **Steps clear** column, and
`StepsTable` a **Runs passed** column with an `infeasible` state tone; both name
a constraint through `constraintNameIn`, beside the rates. The channel
(`react/optimizations/channel/create-optimization-channel/trial-constraints.ts`)
prunes an infeasible draw before any simulation and runs state constraints as
`auxiliaryMetrics` on the detached objective request, 0/1 indicators
aggregated with `min` over each run's frames.

Sensitivity analysis travels the other way. The optimizer core's
`importance.py` estimates PED-ANOVA importances (the one Optuna evaluator that
needs numpy alone) and `pyodide_entry.py` attaches them to the summary and, at
`importance_cadence` past `importance_floor`, to trial events; the worker's
`asImportances` drops a malformed block rather than failing the study, and the
browser capability copies the block onto the `trial` and `complete` log events
as `importances`. `OptimizationsProvider` keeps the latest on
`record.importance`. `ParameterImportancePanel`
(`study-results/parameter-importance-panel.tsx`), the **Sensitivity analysis**
card, renders it from the pure
`importance-view.ts`: one row per optimized parameter in binding order, so no
row moves as estimates land and the bars carry the ranking, a signed Pearson
correlation per parameter computed from `record.trials`, and the fade rule (`tone="muted"`,
bars at a quarter opacity that no longer set the scale) below
`importanceFloor(requestedTrials)`. A remote study has no panel.

The Parameters card (`FrameCard`, the chart card's chrome
(`SimulateView/shared/chart-card.tsx`: `ChartCard`, `ChartCardMenu`,
`ChartCardGrid`, `chartCardHeight`) with the parameters held fixed folded
behind its footer button) carries the state line,
`describeSelection` (`Following step 4`,
`12 of 25 runs — refining`, `Could not compute: …`, or the refinement note)
with the **Follow steps** switch while the study runs; the navigator is a
slider per numeric axis and a toggle per boolean parameter. The controls are
disabled while a running study is followed; every change goes to
`setOptimizationNavigation` as a patch.

`PresentationToggle` (`study-results/study-actions.tsx`) is the footer's left
side (`footerSecondary`), the switch to the other presentation (**Open full
view** in the drawer, **Show in drawer** in the full view, through
`setSimulatePresentation`); pinned to that edge, it never moves as the actions
beside Close come and go. `StudyActions`, the model's footer, offers **Pause**
(primary) and **Stop** for an active
connected study or **Cancel** for a remote one, **Remove** and **Run at the
best configuration** (`refineOptimizationBest`) once a connected study is
settled, `ContinueControl` while `connected.resumable` (a count starting at
the study's own step count and capped by `remainingOptimizationSteps`, the
distance to `PETRINAUT_OPTIMIZATION_MAX_TRIALS`), **Retry** on an error, and
in the drawer **Close**. A paused study gets `PausedStudyActions` instead:
**Resume** (`resumeOptimization`, the requested count minus the steps told,
enabled once `connected.resumable`), **Run at the best configuration**, and a
`ChartCardMenu` ellipsis labelled **More actions** holding **Remove**, so
Remove never sits beside the primary actions; no Restart. The paused body is
the running body with `tone="paused"` on every chart card and the resume note
in the frame's note row. Settling on a pause, a stop or a failure never starts the
point refinement; only a completed study refines where it parks.

## Studies started from a sweep

A parameter sweep's Parameters card (`experiments/sweep-optimize-control.tsx`
over the `useSweepOptimizer` hook in `experiments/sweep-optimizer.ts`) starts
a study with `createOptimization(input, { sweep })`. `buildSweepOptimizationInput`
writes the manifest from the experiment: the swept axes as linear optimize
bindings, every other scenario parameter fixed at its default, the chosen
experiment metric as the sole `Metric`, `seedsPerTrial` at the trial's run
cap. The provider gives such a study an `origin` (`{ kind: "sweep",
experimentId }`), no connected state and no drawer navigation, routes its
run's `evaluateTrial` to a sweep trial evaluator
([react.optimizations.sweep-evaluator](layer:react.optimizations.sweep-evaluator))
in front of the channel, and hides it from this tab's list. The hook exposes
the study, every study started from the sweep oldest first as `studies`, and
`driving`, the step of the study driving the sweep or null. While it drives,
the card takes the `optimizing` tone, its sliders follow the trials disabled,
the same purple button reads Stop and cancels the study, and the experiment
drawer's header reads from the study: **Optimizing**, the steps finished as
the progress bar, `Step N` on the computing batch. Settling parks the sweep,
uncapped, on the best point, or on the point it was trying when Stop ended
it; the navigator's status line then keeps the outcome
(`describeStudyProgress`), the drawer's note row shows a failed study's error,
and the experiment's Cancel stops a driving study first.

Once `studies` is non-empty, `experimentResultsModel` fills the Parameters
band's `below` slot (`ResultsBand` in `shared/results-model.ts`,
rendered after `band.content` in `results-view.tsx`) with
`SweepObjectiveStrip` (`experiments/sweep-objective-strip.tsx`): a disclosure
row over a `Fold` holding the shared `ObjectiveHistoryChart` in purple, kept
while the drawer lives. `buildSweepObjectiveHistory`
(`experiments/sweep-objective-strip/sweep-objective-history.ts`) numbers the
studies' steps end to end by `trials.length`, restarts best-so-far per study
and yields the `xMax` and `dividers` the chart takes: a divider at the first
step of every further study that drew a step or is about to, and the right
edge pinned to the last study's `requestedTrials` while it is active, so the
axis stops at the last step run once Stop or an error ended it. A study that
failed before its first step counts nowhere.

## The steps table

`StepsTable` lists `record.trials` newest first: the step number (Optuna's
trial number plus one), the parameters (`formatParameters`), the objective
(`formatNumber`, `—` for a pruned or failed step) and a state mark: a green
check, an orange filter or a red cross, labelled Complete, Pruned or Failed.
It shows the latest `DISPLAYED_STEPS` (200) with a hint naming the total; the
header's stat columns keep the counts. A step a Stop cut short has no row: the study tells it
failed without an event.

## The connected surface

`NavigatedOptimizationSurface` (`optimization-surface.tsx`) computes nothing
of its own. The field is `trialSurfaceField` (each trial with an objective is
a sample and a dot, the best emphasized, pruned trials muted rings), merged
with `inFlightSurfaceField` (a ring per trial in flight, and a sample too once
it has a running objective) and `navigatedSurfaceSample`, the selection stream's
running value at the navigation: the followed step until its own event lands,
the refined point afterwards. `surfaceInteraction` keeps the plot display-only
(`following`) while a running study is followed and lets a click or drag
commit `setOptimizationNavigation` (`navigable`) once the study is terminal or
following is off; `describeSurfaceState` writes the caption
(`12 steps placed · best 0.36 · the optimizer is choosing the next point`, or
`12 steps · refining the picked point: 8 of 25 runs · drag or click to refine a point`).
The [connected study](doc:optimizer/connected-study) owns the navigation and
the selection stream the surface reads.
