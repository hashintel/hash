`optimizations-view.tsx` is the **Optimizations** tab of Simulate mode. It
reads the [optimizations provider](layer:react.optimizations)'s context and
renders one table row per record (name, scenario, objective with its
direction, steps finished over requested, best value, and a status chip),
with **Create** opening the create drawer
(`setSimulateDrawer({ type: "create-optimization" })`) and a row selecting the
record, which the navigation state carries as the simulate resource and the
view drawer shows. The tab is rendered only while `useOptimizationSource()`
yields a source: a remote capability, or a connected one with the experimental
**In-browser optimization** setting on.

## Status words

`describeOptimizationStatus` names a record's status for the chip and the
drawer: Initializing, Running, Complete, Error, and for `cancelled` either
**Cancelled** (remote) or **Stopped** (connected), because a stopped connected
study keeps its sampler and can be continued. The strip appends
"(reconnecting…)" while a remote stream reconnects.

## The study body: drawer and full view

`study-view.tsx` is the one body both surfaces render. `StudyBody` branches
once, on `record.connected`: a `ConnectedStudyState` renders
`ConnectedStudyBody`, `null` renders `RemoteStudyBody`; `layout` (`drawer` or
`full`) only changes the grid the chart cards sit in and the steps' minimum
height. `view-optimization-drawer.tsx` puts the body in a `Drawer` whose title is one
line (the name, then `describeStudy`) with `StudyActions` in the footer; `optimization-full-view.tsx` gives it the whole
section under a top bar with **Back to list** (clears the simulate resource),
the study's name and `describeStudy`, and the same `StudyActions`.
`optimizations-view.tsx` picks the surface from
`EditorContext.simulatePresentation` (the navigation state's
`simulatePresentation`, `drawer` or `full`, which the website's URL carries as
`present`): `full` with a selected record renders the full view in place of
the list and the drawer.

`StudyBody` is two bands: `StudySummaryBand`
(`study-view/study-summary-strip.tsx`), which holds still, and one scrolling
region beneath it; neither the drawer body nor the full view scrolls on its
own, so the band never moves the body's scroll offset. The band has no title:
it is the header continued, not a section. Its first row is `StudyHeader`
(`study-view/study-header.tsx`):
`describeStudyProgress` (`Step 17 of 30 · best step so far: step 12 (650.5)`
while running; `Finished`, `Stopped`, `Cancelled` or `Failed after …` once
settled) and, while running, a verdict chip from `assessConvergence`
(`study-view/convergence.ts`): best-so-far stagnation over the last
`convergenceWindow(requestedTrials)` completed steps, `too-early`, `improving`
or `converging`. `studyPhase` (`study-view/study-phase.ts`) is `live` while
`isOptimizationActive` and `settled` otherwise; a settled body shows results
and never a live title.

The band continues with the strip: the status with its dot, **Steps** as
`describeStepProgress` (`4 / 30`, then `· 3 runs each` and `· 2 at once` when
those are above 1), and **Best step so far** with the best step's parameters
in a tooltip (`formatParameters`). Beneath it `ComputeActivity` shows the
steps bar (`stepsBar`) and, for a connected study, the followed step's runs as
a thinner bar (`followedStepBar`, `Step 4 · 2 / 3 runs`) and the `N computing`
chip that expands into one row per batch (`describeBatch`: `Step 4`, or
`Refining population=1850, infected_ratio=0.36`). Only a connected study gets
the `ComputeBackendBadge` at the right of the header row and the
`Ran on the CPU: …` fallback note; the record's error text follows for both.

`ObjectiveHistoryCard` (`study-view/objective-history-chart.tsx`) is the
objective by step: `buildObjectiveHistory` (`study-view/objective-history-data.ts`)
orders the trials by step and threads the best so far through the completed
ones, `toObjectiveHistoryData` lays them out as uPlot aligned data
(`[steps, objectives, bestSoFar, infeasibleObjectives]`), and the chart draws
the objectives as dots and the best as a stepped line. `trialFeasibility`
reads a point's feasibility off the trial event's `constraints`: `unknown`
without results, `infeasible` for a draw pruned by a parameter constraint;
an `infeasible` point moves to the fourth series and draws in
`infeasibleColor`, and never becomes the best so far.

A study with constraints reports them through
`react/optimizations/constraint-rates.ts`, derived per render from
`record.trials`: `stepVerdict` (clear, limited, infeasible), `bindingStepRate`
(the step's lowest pass rate) and `studyConstraintRates` (steps clear over
steps simulated, per constraint too). `ConstraintSummaryCard`
(`study-view/constraint-summary.tsx`) is the fourth chart card of a connected
body, `StudySummaryBand` gains a **Steps clear** stat, and `StepsTable` a
**Runs passed** column with an `infeasible` state tone. The channel
(`react/optimizations/channel/create-optimization-channel/trial-constraints.ts`)
prunes an infeasible draw before any simulation and runs state constraints as
`auxiliaryMetrics` on the detached objective request, 0/1 indicators
aggregated with `min` over each run's frames.

Sensitivity analysis travels the other way. The optimizer core's
`importance.py` estimates PED-ANOVA importances (the one Optuna evaluator that
needs numpy alone) and `pyodide_entry.py` attaches them to the summary and, at
`importance_cadence` past `importance_floor`, to trial events; the worker's
`asImportances` drops a malformed block rather than failing the study, and the
browser capability copies the block onto the `trial` and `complete` log events
as `importances`. `OptimizationsProvider` keeps the latest on
`record.importance`. `ParameterImportancePanel`
(`study-view/parameter-importance-panel.tsx`), the **Sensitivity analysis**
card, renders it from the pure
`importance-view.ts`: rows sorted by share, a signed Pearson correlation per
parameter computed from `record.trials`, and the fade rule (`tone="muted"`,
bars at a quarter opacity that no longer set the scale) below
`importanceFloor(requestedTrials)`. A remote study has no panel.

The remote body continues with a collapsible **Best parameters** grid, the
objective by step, the experimental **Surface** section when the
**Optimization surface** setting is on and the study has two or more numeric
axes (`OptimizationSurface`, which walks a grid through
`sampleDetachedObjective`; [the sweep surface](doc:experiments/sweep-surface)
covers it), and a **Steps** section once trials exist.

The connected body is laid out so the strip, the controls and the plots stay
in view while the study streams:

- `NavigatorBand` (`study-view/navigator-band.tsx`): the
  **Parameters** title with its help tooltip, `OptimizationNavigatorStatus`,
  the state line from `describeSelection` (`Following step 4`,
  `12 of 25 runs — refining`, `Could not compute: …`, or the refinement note)
  with the **Follow steps** switch while the study runs, and
  `OptimizationNavigator`, a slider per numeric axis and a toggle per boolean parameter in a grid of
  400 px columns. The controls are disabled while a running study is
  followed; every change goes to `setOptimizationNavigation` as a patch.
- A grid of chart cards: `NavigatedOptimizationSurface` when the study has
  two or more numeric axes, `OptimizationMetrics`, one distribution timeline
  of the objective fed `connected.selection` (the followed step, or the
  refined point), which shows the empty shell when the selection carries an
  error, and `ObjectiveHistoryCard`. The timeline's title is
  `objectiveAtPointTitle`: **Objective at the step in flight** while a live
  study is followed, **Objective at the selected point** otherwise. The
  drawer's grid wraps to two rows and gives a lone last card the whole row;
  the full view's grid holds three in one row. `ConstraintSummaryCard` follows
  when the study declares constraints, and `ParameterImportancePanel` is
  always last.
- `StepsTable` filling the remaining height, the best step starred and tinted
  (`bestTrial`); the remote body passes `bestTrial={null}`.

`StudyActions` offers the switch to the other presentation (**Open full view**
in the drawer, **Show in drawer** in the full view, through
`setSimulatePresentation`), **Remove** once the study is inactive, **Stop**
(connected) or **Cancel** (remote) while it is active, `ContinueControl` while
`connected.resumable` (a count starting at the study's own step count and
capped by `remainingOptimizationSteps`, the distance to
`PETRINAUT_OPTIMIZATION_MAX_TRIALS`), **Retry** on an error, and in the drawer
**Close**.

## The steps table

`StepsTable` lists `record.trials` newest first: the step number (Optuna's
trial number plus one), the parameters (`formatParameters`), the objective
(`formatNumber`, `—` for a pruned or failed step) and a state mark: a green
check, an orange filter or a red cross, labelled Complete, Pruned or Failed.
It shows the latest `DISPLAYED_STEPS` (200) with a hint naming the total; the
strip keeps the counts. A step a Stop cut short has no row: the study tells it
failed without an event.

## The connected surface

`NavigatedOptimizationSurface` (`optimization-surface.tsx`) computes nothing
of its own. The field is `trialSurfaceField` (each trial with an objective is
a sample and a dot, the best emphasized, pruned trials muted rings), merged
with `inFlightSurfaceField` (a ring per trial in flight, and a sample too once
it has a running objective) and `navigatedSurfaceSample`, the selection stream's
running value at the navigation: the followed step until its own event lands,
the refined point afterwards. `surfaceInteraction` keeps the plot display-only
(`following`) while a running study is followed and lets a click or drag
commit `setOptimizationNavigation` (`navigable`) once the study is terminal or
following is off; `describeSurfaceState` writes the caption
(`12 steps placed · best 0.36 · the optimizer is choosing the next point`, or
`12 steps · refining the picked point: 8 of 25 runs · drag or click to refine a point`).
The [connected study](doc:optimizer/connected-study) owns the navigation and
the selection stream the surface reads.
