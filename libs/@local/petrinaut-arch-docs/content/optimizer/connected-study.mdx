A **connected** study is one the tab evaluates itself through the
[browser runtime](doc:optimizer/browser-runtime); a **remote** one runs on the
optimizer service. Both share the event stream and the record fields it
fills. A connected record also carries `record.connected`, a
`ConnectedStudyState`, and this layer is the object that fills it: one
`createConnectedStudy` per record, made by the
[optimizations provider](layer:react.optimizations) when the study is created
and disposed when the record is removed or the connection is disposed.
Everything below happens outside React. The study publishes
`{ navigation, selection, activity, inFlight }` through `onUpdate`, and the
provider patches them onto the record.

## Following

`navigation` is the point the drawer looks at: one quantized position per
optimized numeric parameter (an `OptimizationSurfaceAxis` from
`buildOptimizationSurfaceAxes`), one value per optimized boolean parameter,
and `followTrials`. It starts at each axis's midpoint
(`optimizationAxisMidpoint`), at the scenario's defaults for the booleans, and
with `followTrials: true`.

The channel reports each trial's detached run to the study. `trialStarted`
registers the trial in the activity list and, while following, moves the
navigation to the trial's values (`optimizationAxisPositionFor` per axis) and
mirrors the run's frames and progress stores into `selection` under the key
`trial:<n>`, so the objective's chart beside the drawer's Surface streams the
step being evaluated. With several trials in flight the most recently started
one is followed. `trialSettled` clears the followed selection's `computing`
flag; a failed batch records its reason on `selection.error` (a cancelled one
records nothing), and following moves on to the latest trial still in flight.
`trialReported` keeps the best from each trial event with `foldBestTrial`, so
the refinement below can compare against it.

`setNavigation` applies a patch. A change to a position or a boolean clears
`followTrials` and refines at the new point; a patch that turns `followTrials`
back on stops the refinement and follows the most recently started trial. Once
the study is terminal, every move refines.

## The refinement ladder

`createPointRefinement` (`provider/point-refinement.ts`) refines one point as
the sweep session refines the navigator's selection: cumulative batches up
`EXPERIMENT_RUN_LADDER` (`getNextRunTarget`), capped at
`POINT_REFINEMENT_MAX_RUNS`, one `runDetachedObjective` batch per rung with
`seed = sweepBatchSeed(studySeed, from)` and `runCount = target − from`,
frames merged with `mergeMetricFramesAcrossCells`, and the result cached per
navigation key for the record's lifetime. Each batch runs the study's frozen
snapshot on its requested backend, compiled once under the record id as
`cacheKey`, and registers in the activity list as `{ kind: "refine", values }`
with the point's optimized parameter values. A key change cancels the batch in
flight; returning to a cached key resumes from its finished rungs. A rung that
fails stops the ladder at that key and records the batch's reason on
`selection.error`; the next refine of that key, on a later navigation change,
retries.

Between rungs, `estimateObjective` reads the point's mean and standard error
from the objective's last distribution frame with samples, and
`shouldStopRefining` stops the ladder when the mean sits more than
`STOP_MARGIN_STANDARD_ERRORS` standard errors on the wrong side of the study's
best. The ladder then publishes `selection.note` from `cannotBeatBestNote`
("8 runs · cannot beat the best"). The best trial's own point is exempt
(`isBest`) and climbs to the top rung.

## Settling, Stop and Continue

When the study reaches a terminal status, the provider calls `settle`. While
following is on and no trial is still being followed, the navigation moves to
the best trial's point and refines there; without a completed trial it stays
where it is, with following off. A navigation the user moved earlier stays
put. A trial still followed at that moment settles first, and `trialSettled`
then makes the same move.

**Stop** in the drawer calls the provider's `cancelOptimization`. For a
connected study it calls `cancelOptimizationRun` on the capability and marks
the record cancelled at once (the drawer reads it as **Stopped**), with
`connected.resumable` still false: the worker resolves the steps in flight
first, and the core refuses to extend the run until the segment's terminal
event lands. The attachment stays to apply that event, so the record's cursor
covers the whole segment. The stopped trials in flight are told failed without
an event, so they appear nowhere: in neither the steps table nor the counts.
The terminal event's `resumable` flag sets `connected.resumable`: a segment
that never reached the worker carries `false`, and the footer offers
**Continue** only when it is `true`.

**Continue** calls `extendOptimization(optimizationId, trials)`: the provider
calls `extendOptimizationRun(runId, trials)`, then `resume()` on the study
(`followTrials` back on, the refinement stopped), and re-runs its attach loop
from `record.lastSeq`, so the new `started` event sets the total and the new
trials are followed as they are evaluated. **Remove** calls
`releaseOptimizationRun` and disposes the study: the followed subscriptions
end, every in-flight entry is released, the activity list clears and the
refinement cache is dropped.

## The activity list and the trials in flight

`activity` is a `BatchRegistry` (`react/experiments/shared/batch-registry.ts`)
over two kinds, `trial` before `refine`: every trial in flight and the
refinement rung, each with its `runCount` and `completedRuns`, republished on
a 100 ms throttle as progress ticks. `inFlight` lists every trial being
evaluated with its running objective (`sweepCellObjective` over the run's
frames so far), most recently started last; the Surface draws them as rings.
The [Optimizations tab](doc:ui/optimizations-tab) renders both.
