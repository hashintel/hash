The default (classic) canvas nodes take the compact nodes' colours and shadow. A place is a rounded rectangle and a transition a squared one, both at the same 5:4 box, with the name centred over up to three lines. Their outline is a 1.5px ring, a shade heavier than the compact card's border. A node name that is not written in PascalCase keeps all of its words.
