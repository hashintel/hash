The default (classic) canvas nodes take the compact nodes' colours and shadow. A place is fully rounded at its ends and drawn 10:9, a transition is squared and drawn 3:2, each with its name centred over up to three lines. Their outline is a 1.5px ring, a shade heavier than the compact card's border. A node name that is not written in PascalCase keeps all of its words.
