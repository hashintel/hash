The default (classic) canvas nodes take the compact nodes' colours and shadow. A place is a circle and shows its name over up to three lines. A transition is squared, drawn 3:1, and shows its name on one line. Their outline is a 1.5px ring, a shade heavier than the compact card's border. A node name that is not written in PascalCase keeps all of its words.
