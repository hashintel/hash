A connected optimization source runs studies in this browser behind the experimental In-browser optimization setting. The optimization form gains Runs per step and the experiments' Backend switch, which stays on the CPU because the GPU backend cannot compute an expression objective. A connected study's drawer streams the objective's metrics for the step being evaluated, and for whichever point the navigator or the surface picks once the study is over.
