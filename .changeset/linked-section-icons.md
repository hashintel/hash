Add chain-link, copy-link, and broken-link icons for hyperlinks, copy actions, and disconnected links. Use `HashIcon` with `name="link"`, `"linkCopy"`, or `"linkBroken"`, the corresponding `LinkIcon`, `LinkCopyIcon`, and `LinkBrokenIcon` exports, or `hashIconPack`. The copy-link artwork exposes `data-link-part="copy"` and `data-link-part="chain"` so consumers can animate between the plain chain and copy state.
