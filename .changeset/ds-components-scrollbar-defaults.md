The preset now restyles native scrollbars across its theme scope: scroll containers show a rounded thumb on an invisible track, in light and dark mode. The thumb stays hidden until the container is hovered (faded in and out over 200ms; in Safari the reveal is instant and expressed as direct hover rules on the scrollbar pseudo-elements, since WebKit does not re-resolve scrollbar styles when an inherited custom property changes on the container) and shows in the darker shade while the container is being scrolled — by wheel, keyboard, or programmatically. Textareas and selectable lists use a thinner 8px variant. On systems set to always show scrollbars, thumbs stay visible instead. The reveal-on-scroll tracking and the always-show detection (recorded as a `ds-scrollbars-visible` class on `<html>`) are set up by calling the new `useScrollbarBehavior` hook (or `applyScrollbarBehavior`) once from the component rendering the theme scope root. Firefox and other engines without `::-webkit-scrollbar` support fall back to thin standard scrollbars with the same behavior.
