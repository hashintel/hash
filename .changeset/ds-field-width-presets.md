`Select`'s `width` presets now include its arrow, so a `Select` with a preset `width` is narrower and exactly as wide as a `TextInput` of the same `width`. `TagsInput` and a `multiple` `Combobox` now keep their preset `width` too, where they used to grow with their values and stretch to fill a flex-column or grid parent.
